Example projects

  • Lead routing. Send website enquiries to a shared inbox or CRM, preserving the requested service and contact details.
  • Record synchronization. Map fields between tools and define which system owns each field so updates and retries don’t create duplicate records.
  • Reporting pipelines. Collect operational data from approved tools, validate required fields, and alert the team when a sync fails.

How we work

We review the systems, permissions, and data formats, then agree on field mappings and ownership. Before launch, we test normal updates, duplicates, and failures so your team knows what to do when a connection needs attention.
